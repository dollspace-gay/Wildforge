# Working in deterministic generation

Read [README.md](README.md) and [the source guidance](../AGENTS.md).
Stage inputs and outputs must be explicit. Preserve stage and iteration order,
seed derivation, seam behavior, and finite water/material quantities in moves.
Keep scheduling, persistence, network, and presentation outside generation.
Do not tune world appearance as part of structural extraction. Target 400 lines
and review above 500; add local guides for any further maintained directory.
