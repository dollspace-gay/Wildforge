# Working in geology

Read [README.md](README.md) and [the atlas guidance](../AGENTS.md).
Preserve operation order, seed salts, sphere topology, enum representations,
provider/site IDs, and finite deposit/chamber budgets. Do not loosen attempt
constraints or alter the existing fixture policy during structural extraction.
Keep generation independent of rendering, networking, and world mutation.
Queries read the selected manifests; only the genesis coordinator selects them.
Use explicit imports and geology-scoped helpers. Aim for 400 lines per cohesive
module and review above 500. Run focused geology/atlas/codec scenarios and the
applicable Rust/runtime gates before acceptance.
